Latest CZ Chatbot UX note: Home suggested-topic clicks now use the host-provided structured resolver, so `How much can I save?`, `Review today's money snapshot`, `What products can I open`, `Review latest 5 transactions`, and `Spot unusual spending` produce page-aware CZ money/product/transaction replies with rich cards and follow-up chips instead of the generic fallback answer. The savings-capacity topic derives a mock monthly target from the Spending timeline and current-account cash, explains income, expenses, and balances, presents Saving account at `3.5% p.a.` and Term deposit at `5% p.a.`, asks for an amount, and ends with an `Open now` handoff to the selected Product detail page. Its final `Ready to open` answer and presentation card now calculate the selected amount against the selected product rate: Saving account shows an approximate monthly interest amount, while Term deposit shows an approximate annual interest amount. Its capacity card is positioned between the intro copy and the saving-choice card, uses vertical icon rows with right-aligned amounts, and reuses existing colored PFM icons (`Income`, `Shopping`, `Finance`, `Wallet`, `Investments`). The saving product cards are compact two-column presentation cards with no direct click path; selection stays in the follow-up chip shelf, connected by `Choose your preferred saving type.` The savings path intentionally does not expose an `Open Spending` action from the capacity card or follow-up chips, and chatbot-visible wording now says `simulation` instead of `demo`. Chat streaming now preserves the user's reading position after they scroll away from the bottom, instead of forcing the conversation down when the assistant finishes rendering. The product-opening topic is grounded in the Products `OUR PRODUCTS` shelf config and sheet options, with an `Open Products` handoff to `screen=products`; each rich product card also closes chat, selects Products / Banking, scrolls to `OUR PRODUCTS`, and opens the matching Products bottom sheet. The transaction topics reuse the Spending timeline/source transaction data to list the five latest transactions with signed amounts and source accounts, and to highlight largest recent outgoing movement, top money-out category, and pending items. Investments suggested topics now show `Start an investment goal`, `Review portfolio context`, `Review my orders`, and `Plan next investment move`, with structured replies grounded in mock portfolio value/performance, currency/asset exposure, holding concentration, and executed/pending/rejected order status. The default Level 1 new-conversation entry keeps the internal simulated home-indicator pill hidden while preserving safe bottom spacing; the attempted Gemini-like bottom glow was removed after visual review because it washed the full assistant surface blue. Conversation-list navigation now tracks whether the list was opened from new chat or a selected conversation, shows `OneAI` in the list header, highlights the selected conversation only when appropriate, returns with a right-facing arrow, and keeps conversation detail close separate from the `More options` menu.

Latest CZ Chatbot sales note: the second assistant tab is now `For you`, replacing the old static Discovery-only feed with a hybrid contextual surface. The first implemented mock CRM scenario is Credit Card Detail: a contextual opportunity card above card transactions can open `For you`, while the floating AI launcher opens clean chat and the `For you` segment button carries the red-dot attention cue when opportunities exist; the Card Detail nudge now uses commercial offer copy (`Upgrade your credit limit to 15 000 CZK`) while keeping `FIND OUT MORE` unchanged, the primary opportunity is titled `New credit limit for you`, presents a plafon change from `10 000 CZK` to `15 000 CZK` instead of available-credit availability, includes a related card row with shared Meniga-mapped credit-card artwork/name/masked number, uses a clean non-gradient/non-stroke primary surface, hides internal CRM trigger copy, uses a centered shared `LinkButton` CTA labeled `I'm interested`, removes weak secondary protection/repayment cards, shortens the offer body for the current phone viewport, titles the top offer section `Personalized offers for you`, and organizes the supporting content into `Grow your money`, `Next best conversations`, and `Decide with confidence` categories so the surface communicates product-action conversation starters instead of generic editorial reads. The `Grow your money` hero and `Next best conversations` cards are now pointer drag/swipe-enabled horizontal scroll-snap carousels with additional hidden-behind topics, while `Decide with confidence` lists four guidance topics; the Help entry still opens clean support chat topics. After accepting the credit-limit offer in chat, the follow-up shelf now shows `After acceptance` plus `Sign now`; choosing `After acceptance` returns a single `Sign now` option, and the signing answer explains final terms, strong customer authentication, and signature before activation. Share/browser/PWA metadata now exposes the platform as `Mobile Banking CEE`.

Latest card runtime note: generated and static mock card products now place credit cards before debit cards globally across Baseline/Future and all countries, with two debit cards in the default demo portfolio. Generated current accounts use deterministic per-index balances, and generated debit cards link by index to generated current accounts (`Debit Card 1` mirrors `Primary Account 1`, `Debit Card 2` mirrors `Primary Account 2`) instead of every debit card falling back to the first account. Credit cards display `availableCredit` as the product/card amount after country-currency conversion, keeping Home and Card Detail `Free To Spend` correlated, while debit and meal cards still mirror their linked current account balance. Card Detail, Card Component, and Flow Library card previews use the shared Meniga-mapped `Card` artwork, with credit cards rendering `mc-credit-partner-standard`, the first debit card rendering `mc-debit-gold`, and the second debit card rendering `mc-debit-standard`; the old card SVG image assets were removed and the Apple Wallet CTA is no longer shown. The shared `Card` artwork now uses a single outer rounded mask, container-level fill, and `preserveAspectRatio="none"` so scaled runtime slots do not show white corner or letterbox artifacts.

What this platform actually is:

This repository is a `Vite + React` single-page demo shell for showcasing a UniCredit CEE mobile banking experience across product, country, language, scenario, release, design-system, feature-flag, and separated future-flow preview combinations. It behaves like an interactive prototype with stateful screen transitions, localized copy, static mock data, country-gated visual flows, a mock-driven Flow Library for future release previews such as RO Round Up and RO Card PIN, a Vercel demo access gate, active Mobile PI Kids runtime concepts only for Slovakia and Hungary, and honest planned-state placeholders for unsupported Kids contexts. Romania Kids and Serbia Kids runtime apps were deleted on 2026-07-07 rather than hidden.
